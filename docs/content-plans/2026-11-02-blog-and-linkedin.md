## LinkedIn caption draft

“Premium quality” is not a product specification.

If you sell online, choose one product page and check:

• Can a buyer find its size, material and compatibility?
• Does the selected variation match the photo?
• Do the price and stock agree with your product feed?
• Are delivery and returns clear before checkout?

These details make a page easier to understand—for shoppers and the systems helping them compare products.

Before adding another AI tool, fix the information it would have to work with.
