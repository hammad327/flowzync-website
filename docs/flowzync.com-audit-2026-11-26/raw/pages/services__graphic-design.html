FAQ

Graphic Design — common questions

How many logo concepts do I get?

Three original directions in the first round. You pick one and we refine it until it’s right — refinement rounds on your chosen concept are unlimited.

Do I own the final designs?

Fully. On final payment you receive all source files (AI, Figma, PSD) and complete ownership — no licensing games, no recurring fees.

Can you match my existing brand style?

Absolutely. Send us your current materials and we’ll design new assets that feel like they were always part of the family.

What file formats will I receive?

Everything: vector (AI, SVG, EPS, PDF) for print and scaling, plus PNG/JPG/WebP exports sized for web and social — with transparent versions included.
