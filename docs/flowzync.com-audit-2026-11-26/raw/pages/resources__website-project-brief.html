Who is this brief for?

Business owners, marketing teams and freelancers planning a new website, redesign, WordPress store, GoHighLevel funnel or automation project.

Do I need to know the technology first?

No. Describe the customer journey and the systems you already use. A technical recommendation can follow once the requirements are clear.

Does completing this form request a quote?

No. It creates a brief you can keep or share with your chosen provider. To discuss a project with Flowzync, use the contact page separately.

Can I share the template with a client or colleague?

Yes. Share the page or blank template. If you reference it in your own guide, a link to this page helps readers find the current version.
