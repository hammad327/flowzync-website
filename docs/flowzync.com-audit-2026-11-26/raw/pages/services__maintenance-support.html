FAQ

Maintenance & Support — common questions

Do you support sites you didn’t build?

Yes. New care-plan clients get an onboarding audit, so we know your site inside-out before taking responsibility for it.

What counts as a "small edit"?

Anything under ≈30 minutes: text changes, image swaps, adding a testimonial, tweaking a section. Larger work is quoted separately at plan-holder rates.

How fast do you respond to emergencies?

Response windows and escalation arrangements are agreed in the care plan. Tell us about your support requirements so we can confirm the coverage and responsibilities before you subscribe.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — plans are month-to-month with no lock-in. We keep clients by being useful, not by contract.
