Editorial Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Who publishes our articles

Flowzync publishes the blog as an organisation. The byline identifies the studio rather than implying that a specialist reviewer has independently checked every article. Our writing concerns website design, WordPress, e-commerce, CRM, automation and search visibility.

Sources and opinions

For platform behaviour, search requirements and regulatory statements, we link to relevant first-party documentation where available. Recommendations, design choices and worked examples are Flowzync opinions, not rules issued by a search engine or regulator. Prices and timelines depend on scope, location, taxes, vendors and the date checked; illustrative budgets are not binding quotes or market surveys.

Updates and corrections

We retain the original publication date and show an updated date when substantive content changes. We do not change dates merely to make an article look new. To report an error, send the page URL and the disputed statement to info@flowzync.com. We review correction requests and update the article when a correction is warranted.

Tools and responsibility

Research and drafting may use software and AI assistance. Flowzync is responsible for what it publishes. Generated wording, a score from an SEO tool or a search snippet is not treated as proof of an outcome. Readers should verify current product prices and account-specific functionality with the vendor.

Commercial relationship

Flowzync sells services discussed in the blog. Links to our own services explain how to work with us and should be read in that context. Paid placements and affiliate relationships, if introduced, must be disclosed beside the relevant content. We do not promise Google rankings, AI citations, indexing or AdSense approval.

Reviews, people and project claims

Client quotes need permission and a traceable source before publication. Performance claims need records identifying what was measured and over what period. Portfolio pages describe the presented work and do not imply independent verification of financial or conversion results. Stock photography is illustrative and must not be presented as a photograph of our actual team.

Scope of care and compliance articles

Articles about care providers explain website and software decisions. They are not legal, clinical or regulatory advice for an individual organisation. Consult the current regulator guidance and an appropriate professional for your service, territory and data processing arrangements.
