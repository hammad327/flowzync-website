## Findings

### Critical
None. Nothing prevents AI systems from crawling, rendering or citing the site. The expired www certificate is already logged in CONTEXT.md. It matters here too: any third-party mention that links `https://www.flowzync.com` fails TLS for crawlers, which wastes exactly the off-site citations GEO depends on.

### High

**H1. Flowzync is never defined in one self-contained sentence on the pages AI systems treat as entity sources (home, about, llms.txt summary).**
- Evidence:
  - The home hero (app/page.jsx / components/HomeHero.jsx) is "Custom websites. First impressions that last." with no subject.
  - /about opens "We're a remote studio of senior designers…" and has no "Flowzync is" sentence.
  - The llms.txt blockquote (lib/site.js `site.description`) ends with the tagline fluff "Thoughtfully connected by Flowzync." and does not mention the care niche.
  - The only definitional sentences on the whole site are in FAQs and the per-city "Flowzync is a remote studio working with London clients…".
- Why it matters: assistants answer "What is Flowzync?" and "Which agency does web design and GoHighLevel?" from one extractable sentence. When none exists, they paraphrase from fragments or from city pages.
- Fix (copy uses only facts already published on the site):
  - lib/site.js `description`: "Flowzync is a remote web design and GoHighLevel studio. It builds WordPress, Elementor, WooCommerce and custom websites and connects them to GoHighLevel funnels, CRM and automated follow-up, for clients worldwide, including a dedicated GoHighLevel setup for home care agencies."
  - Home hero subline, or a short visible "About Flowzync" paragraph directly under the hero:
    > Flowzync is a remote web design and GoHighLevel studio. We build the website — WordPress, Elementor or custom code — and the system behind it: GoHighLevel forms, booking calendars, pipelines and automated follow-up, planned as one project. We work with clients worldwide, and we run a dedicated GoHighLevel setup for home care agencies. Every project is quoted against a written, itemised scope.
  - /about: open with the same definition before the values copy.

## What works
- Every AI search and assistant crawler is explicitly allowed, with a documented one-line opt-out (app/robots.js).
- Content is fully server-rendered. Every sampled passage was extracted from raw HTML without JavaScript.
- llms.txt is generated from site data, links all 188 URLs' worth of sections, and carries explicit anti-hallucination guidance (no offices in listed cities, no invented comparisons, no price list).
- Location pages answer "Are you a [city] agency?" with a plain "No. Flowzync is a remote studio…". This is the right defence against location misattribution.
- The care posts are some of the most citable writing on the site: honest, answer-first in most cases, with clear boundaries ("does not replace rostering, care planning or eMAR") and scoped disclaimers.
- 65 of 70 posts include a "Sources and scope" block linking first-party documentation (HighLevel pricing, ICO special category data). Visible published and updated dates match the schema. /editorial-policy explains authorship and corrections.
- FAQ answers are mostly 40–70 words and self-contained: ideal extraction units.
- IndexNow is implemented for fast Bing and Copilot discovery.
