New websites don't need advanced SEO. They need the fundamentals done properly — because most sites never get even those right.

## Days 1–7: make sure Google can read you

- Submit your sitemap in Google Search Console
- Check every important page is indexable (no stray "noindex" tags)
- Fix any pages that return errors
- Make sure the mobile experience works — Google indexes mobile-first

None of this is glamorous. All of it is mandatory.

## Days 7–30: one page, one purpose

Every service you offer deserves its **own page** with its own title, heading and 500+ words of genuinely useful content. A single "Services" page listing everything is asking Google to rank you for nothing in particular.

Write titles the way people search: "Custom Website Design Services" will always beat "What We Do ✨".

## Days 30–60: earn your structure

- Add FAQ sections to service pages (and FAQ schema, so they can appear in rich results)
- Interlink related pages naturally — service to service, blog to service
- Publish your first two or three articles answering real questions your customers ask

## Days 60–90: consistency beats intensity

One useful article a week for a year outperforms twenty articles published in a burst and then silence. Search engines — and readers — reward the habit, not the sprint.

## What to ignore for now

Domain authority scores, keyword density percentages, and anyone promising "#1 rankings guaranteed." Foundations first; the rest is noise until you have traffic to optimise.

> SEO for a new site isn't a trick. It's making it effortless for Google to understand who you help and how.
