Your opening paragraph. Answer the question in your title directly and
completely, right here. Two or three sentences.

Then a sentence or two of context on why it matters, so the reader knows
they're in the right place.

## Your first heading — phrase it as a search

Write normally. Blank line between paragraphs.

Key points work well as bullets:

- Something specific and factual
- Another concrete point
- A third

**Bold** for emphasis, *italic* sparingly.

## A second heading

Link naturally to a relevant service:
[custom website design](/services/custom-website-design), or an industry
page like [healthcare websites](/industries/healthcare-and-medical-practices).

> A blockquote, for a point worth pausing on.

## Practical steps, if the topic suits them

1. First thing to do
2. Second thing
3. Third

## The realistic summary

Close with the honest takeaway — what to actually do, and what not to
bother with. Readers remember the post that told them what to skip.
