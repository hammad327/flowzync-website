A cleaner opens an enquiry that says “How much?” There is no postcode, no property size and no indication of whether the person wants a regular clean or an end-of-tenancy clean. A better form gets enough detail for a useful reply without asking the customer to do the estimator’s job.

## Decide what your first reply needs

Ask the person who answers enquiries to pull up five recent conversations. Write down the details they had to request a second time. A cleaner might need the service type, postcode, number of bedrooms and preferred week. A roofer may need the postcode and whether the problem is active. Those are two different forms.

On a phone, let people select the service and enter their postcode before they type a long description. Provide an “I’m not sure” option where customers may not know the answer. Ask for a photo only if it changes what happens next and explain what to photograph.

## Show what happens after submission

Say who will reply and give a response window your team can meet. If a job is urgent, offer a phone route during the hours you answer it. Run one test with a postcode you serve and one outside your area. Confirm the team receives both with enough information to respond.
