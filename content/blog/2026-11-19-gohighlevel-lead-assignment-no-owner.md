A quote request arrives just before closing. The CRM creates the contact and sends an acknowledgement. The next morning, everyone assumes someone else is following up. Check whether the enquiry ever got an owner.

## Trace one request from the form

Create a test contact for each meaningful entry route. Check who owns the contact and the opportunity, if your pipeline uses both. Confirm the correct person receives a notification and has a clear next action. Repeat with a returning lead. Some workflows are designed to keep the existing owner, which may be right for an active relationship.

## Handle the request that fits no rule

Define a fallback owner for missing service choices, unknown postcodes and staff absence. Give that person a view of unassigned leads and a regular time to review it. Test a service that should go to one named team member and another that should enter the fallback. [HighLevel’s workflow resources](https://help.gohighlevel.com/) can help you map these rules to your available actions.

After launch, sample a handful of enquiries against the form, CRM and notification. The check is complete when someone can see the lead and knows when to respond.
